Do Word files kyun?

        
Editable wali mein text type ki tarah rehta hai — copy, edit, search. Visual wali mein original page ki photo Word ke andar baithti hai, isliye dikhawat same-to-same hoti hai.

      

      

        
Kab OCR, kab asli text?

        
Agar PDF mein pehle se text hai to wahi nikalte hain — Hindi headings, tables, numbers sab. Scanned photo ya screenshot ho to OCR se padhte hain.

      

      

        
Kitna same?

        
Tables, headings, alignment aur spacing recreate hote hain. Complicated design (watermarks, odd columns) ke liye visual Word + PNG use karo.
